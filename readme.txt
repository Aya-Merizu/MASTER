PROJECT DISTRIBUTION SUSPENDED
===============================================================================

This project and all associated source code repositories have been intentionally
withdrawn from public distribution.

REASON FOR SUSPENSION:
----------------------
The traditional open-source social contract was built on the principle of mutual
respect, transparent collaboration, and fair reciprocity between developers and 
the wider community.

In recent years, this foundation has been fundamentally eroded. Major artificial 
intelligence corporations routinely scrape, index, and commodify publicly available 
software repositories under the shelter of "Fair Use" doctrines and ambiguous 
Text and Data Mining (TDM) legal exceptions. 

Independent creators invest hundreds of hours into complex architectural design, 
low-level engineering, and original implementations, only to have their labor 
absorbed into commercial proprietary models without meaningful consent, fair 
compensation, or direct attribution.

Current copyright laws and open-source licensing mechanisms lack the enforceable 
tools necessary to protect independent creators from non-consensual automated 
harvesting by centralized corporate entities.

FUTURE AVAILABILITY:
--------------------
Until robust legal and technical frameworks exist to guarantee absolute intellectual 
property protection and prevent predatory exploitation of public source code, 
this project will remain in private development.

All previous branches, commit histories, and public source files have been permanently 
purged from this public repository.
